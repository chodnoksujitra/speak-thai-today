### [PHRASE 1: 0:40–1:50] สวัสดีค่ะ / สวัสดีครับ (Hello)

**Pronunciation**: 
- Female: สวัสดีค่ะ = "Sa-wat-dee KHA"
- Male: สวัสดีครับ = "Sa-wat-dee KHRAP"

"Phrase number one — and this one you probably already know — is 'Hello.' In Thai, that's สวัสดี — sa-wat-dee.

But here's what most people get wrong. In Thai, you always add a polite word at the end. If you're a woman, you say: สวัสดีค่ะ — sa-wat-dee KHA. If you're a man, you say: สวัสดีครับ — sa-wat-dee KHRAP.

These little words — ค่ะ and ครับ — they're like the Thai version of 'ma'am' and 'sir.' They show respect. And Thai people LOVE it when foreigners use them.

Let's practice. Repeat after me:
- สวัสดีค่ะ... สวัสดีค่ะ...
- สวัสดีครับ... สวัสดีครับ...

Perfect! Use this phrase every time you walk into a shop, a restaurant, a hotel — anywhere. You'll see people's faces change immediately."

---

### [PHRASE 2: 1:50–3:00] ขอบคุณมากๆ ค่ะ/ครับ (Thank You Very Much)

**Pronunciation**: 
- Female: ขอบคุณค่ะ = "khob-khun KHA"
- Male: ขอบคุณครับ = "khob-khun KHRAP"

"Phrase number two — Thank you. In Thai: ขอบคุณ — khob-khun. Add your polite word: ขอบคุณค่ะ or ขอบคุณครับ.

This phrase is your secret weapon. Every time you tip, every time someone helps you, every time you buy something — use it. And watch how their reaction changes."

### [PHRASE 3: 3:00–4:10] เท่าไหร่ครับ? (How Much Is This?)

**Pronunciation**: "tao-rai KHRAP?"

"Phrase number three is my personal favorite — and it will save you money. 'How much is this?' In Thai: เท่าไหร่ — tao-rai.

The vendor will immediately know you've done your homework. You're not just another tourist who points and pulls out your wallet."

### [PHRASE 5: 5:10–6:00] ไม่เป็นไร (No Worries / It's Okay)

**Pronunciation**: "mai pen rai"

It means 'It's okay,' 'No worries,' 'Never mind,' 'Don't stress about it.'"
