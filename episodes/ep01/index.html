Full Transcript

0:00 – 0:40
Introduction: Welcome to Speak Thai Today! I'm your host, and today we're going to learn 5 essential Thai phrases that will completely change how you experience Thailand. Whether you're visiting Bangkok, exploring Chiang Mai, or just meeting Thai speakers, these phrases will help you connect with locals and navigate daily situations with confidence.
0:40 – 1:50
Phrase 1: Hello/Goodbye - สวัสดีค่ะ/ครับ (Sa-wat-dee kha/khrap)

This is probably the most important phrase you'll learn. "Sawasdee" means both hello and goodbye in Thai. When you say it, you'll notice two different endings: "kha" (ค่ะ) if you're female, and "khrap" (ครับ) if you're male. These are politeness particles that show respect. Let me teach you the proper way to say it with the traditional wai gesture.
1:50 – 3:00
Phrase 2: Thank you very much - ขอบคุณมากๆ ค่ะ/ครับ (Khob-khun maak maak kha/khrap)

"Khob khun" means thank you. When you add "maak maak," you're saying "thank you very much." This phrase is essential because Thai people truly appreciate gratitude. Whether you're thanking someone for directions, a meal, or service, using this phrase with the proper politeness particle will win hearts.
3:00 – 4:10
Phrase 3: How much is this? - เท่าไหร่ครับ? (Tao-rai khrap?)

When you're shopping at the night market or trying local street food, you'll definitely need this phrase. "Tao rai" literally translates to "how much." This simple question can save you from confusion and help you haggle at markets. Remember, in many Thai markets, negotiating prices is expected and can be fun!
4:10 – 5:10
Phrase 4: Delicious! - อร่อยมาก! (A-roi maak!)

Thailand has some of the most amazing food in the world, and Thai people love compliments about their cuisine. "Aroy" means delicious, and "maak" means very. When you say "aroy maak," you're telling people the food is really delicious. This will instantly make you popular at any family dinner or restaurant!
5:10 – 6:00
Phrase 5: No worries / It's okay - ไม่เป็นไร (Mai pen rai)

This phrase is incredibly useful because it can mean "no worries," "it's okay," "never mind," or "you're welcome." It's one of the most versatile phrases in Thai. Use it when someone apologizes, when you want to say you don't mind, or as a cool, relaxed response. It's the go-to phrase for casual Thai conversations.
6:00 – 6:40
Outro: Congratulations! You've just learned 5 essential phrases that will truly transform your experience in Thailand. Practice saying these phrases out loud—pronunciation and the tone of voice matter. Thai people are incredibly welcoming and will appreciate your effort to speak their language. Subscribe to Speak Thai Today for more lessons every week. Come back next time as we dive deeper into Thai greetings and learn the proper way to show respect in different situations. Thank you, and I'll see you next time!
