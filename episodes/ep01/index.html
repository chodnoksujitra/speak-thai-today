5 Thai Phrases That Will Change Your Trip to Thailand

Five simple phrases — and Thai people's faces light up. Learn how to say hello, thank you, how much, delicious and the famous ไม่เป็นไร.

The 5 phrases

Women end with ค่ะ (kha), men end with ครับ (khrap) — the Thai "ma'am" and "sir".

▶ 0:401Hello / Goodbye
สวัสดีค่ะ / ครับ
Sa-wat-dee kha / khrap

One word for both hello and goodbye — say it with a wai 🙏

USEwalking into a shop, hotel, restaurant
▶ 1:502Thank you very much
ขอบคุณมากๆ ค่ะ / ครับ
Khob-khun maak maak kha / khrap

ขอบคุณ = thank you · มากๆ = very, very much

USEafter help, a meal, a tip, buying something
▶ 3:003How much is this?
เท่าไหร่ครับ / คะ?
Tao-rai khrap / kha?

เท่าไหร่ = how much — vendors know you've done your homework

USEnight markets, street food, taxis
▶ 4:104Delicious!
อร่อยมาก!
A-roi maak!

อร่อย = delicious · มาก = very — might even get you extra food 😋

USEto the street food vendor or your Thai host
▶ 5:105No worries / It's okay
ไม่เป็นไร
Mai pen rai

Not just a phrase — a whole Thai way of life: "it's okay, never mind, don't stress, you're welcome".

USEwhen someone says sorryUSEwhen someone says thank youUSEwhen plans change 😌

The polite-word formula

Add one little word to the end of almost any Thai sentence to sound polite.

WOMENphrase+ค่ะ kha
MENphrase+ครับ khrap
Teacher's tip: say สวัสดีค่ะ / ครับ every time you walk in, and ขอบคุณค่ะ / ครับ every time you leave. Those two alone will change how people treat you in Thailand. 💛

0:00 · Intro

Hey there! Welcome back to Speak Thai Today. If you're planning a trip to Thailand — or you're already there — I have something that will change EVERYTHING about your experience.

Five simple Thai phrases. That's it. But here's the thing: when Thai people hear you say these phrases, their whole face changes. They immediately realize you actually cared enough to learn their language.

So grab a notebook, or just keep watching. By the end of this video, you'll have five phrases that are going to make your trip unforgettable. Let's go!

0:40 · Phrase 1 — สวัสดีค่ะ / ครับ (Hello)

Phrase number one — and this one you probably already know — is "Hello." In Thai, that's สวัสดี — sa-wat-dee.

But here's what most people get wrong. In Thai, you always add a polite word at the end. If you're a woman, you say: สวัสดีค่ะ — sa-wat-dee KHA. If you're a man, you say: สวัสดีครับ — sa-wat-dee KHRAP.

These little words — ค่ะ and ครับ — they're like the Thai version of "ma'am" and "sir." They show respect. And Thai people LOVE it when foreigners use them.

Let's practice. Repeat after me: สวัสดีค่ะ... สวัสดีครับ... Perfect! Use this phrase every time you walk into a shop, a restaurant, a hotel — anywhere.

1:50 · Phrase 2 — ขอบคุณมากๆ (Thank you very much)

Phrase number two — Thank you. In Thai: ขอบคุณ — khob-khun. Add your polite word: ขอบคุณค่ะ or ขอบคุณครับ.

This phrase is your secret weapon. Every time you tip, every time someone helps you, every time you buy something — use it. And watch how their reaction changes.

3:00 · Phrase 3 — เท่าไหร่ (How much is this?)

Phrase number three is my personal favorite — and it will save you money. "How much is this?" In Thai: เท่าไหร่ — tao-rai.

The vendor will immediately know you've done your homework. You're not just another tourist who points and pulls out your wallet.

4:10 · Phrase 4 — อร่อยมาก (Delicious!)

Phrase number four is the one that will make every street food vendor love you. "Delicious!" In Thai: อร่อย — a-roi.

I promise you, they will smile so big. It might even get you extra food!

5:10 · Phrase 5 — ไม่เป็นไร (No worries)

And phrase number five — this one is very special because it's not just a phrase. It's a whole Thai philosophy. ไม่เป็นไร — mai pen rai.

It means "It's okay," "No worries," "Never mind," "Don't stress about it."

6:00 · Outro

Take these 5 phrases to Thailand — and you will have a completely different experience from every other tourist.

If this video helped you, please subscribe — I post new Thai lessons every week. Until then — ขอบคุณมากๆ ค่ะ — thank you so much, and I'll see you in the next video! หวัดดี! 👋
