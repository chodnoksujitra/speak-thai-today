BOOK 2 · LESSON 14

Silent Leaders: ห and อ

silent ห and อ in front · ฆ ฬ · a Thai BBQ dinner · you can read หิวไหม

AudioAnswers
Book pages 53–66 (with Mixed Review 3)23 audio tracks

By the end of this lesson you can

  • Listen: hear มา / ม้า / หมา and ไหม / ใหม่ / ไม้
  • Speak: ask “Are you hungry?”, invite a friend to eat, and say what you want to eat
  • Read: silent ห and อ in front · ฆ ฬ · 19 new words and a 6-line story
  • Write: put ห or อ in the right place, and 5 words from dictation
  • Rule: ห + a low letter: ห is silent, and the word follows the high-class rules
