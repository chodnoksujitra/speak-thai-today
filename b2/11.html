  • Listen: hear the new short-vowel pairs, 4 out of 5

  • Speak: ask if the bank is closed, ask where to change money, ask if it’s far
  • Write: อึ อือ in the right spot, ธ ภ, and 5 words from dictation
  • Tone: the new short vowels use the Book 1 short-vowel rule
