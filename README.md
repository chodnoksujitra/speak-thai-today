# 🗣️ Speak Thai Today

Learn Thai language and culture the fun and easy way! 

**Speak Thai Today** is a YouTube channel dedicated to teaching Thai language to non-native speakers. We break down Thai phrases, vocabulary, and cultural insights into bite-sized, engaging videos that make learning Thai accessible and enjoyable.

## 📺 Channel Information

- **Platform**: YouTube
- **Niche**: Thai Language Learning | Thai Culture | Speak Thai Easy | Thai for Travelers
- **Upload Schedule**: 2 episodes per week
- **Target Audience**: Beginners and travelers interested in learning Thai

## 🎯 Mission

Our mission is to help non-native speakers master the Thai language while understanding Thai culture deeply. Whether you're planning a trip to Thailand or want to connect with Thai friends, we've got you covered!

## 📈 Business Goals

- **Target Revenue**: $3,000 - $5,000/month
- **Timeline**: 12 months
- **Revenue Streams**:
  - 💰 YouTube AdSense & Sponsorships
  - 📚 KDP Thai Learning Workbooks
  - 🎓 Thai Learning App

## 📚 Content Categories

- **Thai Phrases & Expressions**: Essential phrases for daily life
- **Thai Culture**: Understanding Thai customs and traditions
- **Survival Thai**: Practical Thai for travelers
- **Thai Vocabulary**: Building your Thai word bank
- **Native Speaker Tips**: Learning from a native perspective
