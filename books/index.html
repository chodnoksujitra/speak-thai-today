Meet the author

Sujitra Lorsuwan (Teacher Bua) is a native Thai teacher from Bangkok. She writes for adult beginners and teaches the way she does in class: one sound, one letter, one real word at a time. She also makes free Thai lessons on YouTube (Speak Thai Today).

She has published Thai learning books since 2022. Earlier titles appear on Amazon under the name zuzira books. Our Start Here page shows which book to pick.
