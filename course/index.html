Questions

How many minutes or days do I need?

The course is built from short video lessons plus a printable worksheet for each one, so you can work in small pieces. A little every day works better than one long session a month. The exact lesson lengths and a day-by-day schedule have not been announced yet.

Do I need to know any Thai first?

No. Level 1 starts from zero. The first lessons teach you to read your first words and hear the 5 Thai tones.

Are there worksheets?

Yes. The free preview lessons come with a printable worksheet. Worksheets for the later lessons are being prepared. Join the email list to get the free worksheets and the launch news.

Who checks the Thai?

The course is taught by Teacher Bua, and the Thai content is reviewed by Thai teachers before it is published.

When will the course be on sale, and how much will it cost?

The sale date, the price and the platform have not been announced yet. Lessons 3 to 8 are still being made. Join the email list and you will hear first.

How is this related to the Teacher Bua books on Amazon?

The course follows the same step-by-step path as Book 1 of Learn Thai with Teacher Bua (letters, sounds and first words). You do not need the book to follow the free preview lessons, and the book is a good way to practise on paper.
