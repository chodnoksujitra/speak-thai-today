The same answers as in your book, the right way up. Tap a line to show it. Finish the page first!

p.45 Guess first

เจ jee = vegan food (no meat, fish, egg, milk; no garlic or onion). Look for this flag if you don’t eat meat.

p.45 Warm-up

1 crab · 2 low · 3 no air · 4 five

p.46

1 juun · 2 the woman · 3 too (mʉ̌an-gan) · Telling or asking: 1 kâ · 2 ká · 3 ká · 4 kâ

p.48

Rule 1 check: gɛɛ · Rule 2 check: mid · Blend: ใจ jai · แก gɛɛ

p.49

Odd one out: ใป (not a word) · ไจ (not a word) · ตอ (stump, Lesson 3) · First sound: dt · j · bp · g · Sound Gym: 1 ká · 2 kâ · 3 kâ · 4 ká · 5 kâa · 6 káa · 7 káa · 8 kâa

p.50 Story

Grandpa is kind. Grandpa goes to look at the crabs. The crabs have big eyes.

p.51 Pattern
