Letters and Numbers

7 new letters · Thai numbers 0–10 · room and floor

AudioAnswersExtras

By the end of this lesson you can

  • Listen: hear Thai numbers 1–10 and write them down, 8 out of 10
  • Read: 7 new letters, 12 words, a story and Thai numerals ๐–๙
  • Speak: say your room number and your floor
  • Write: the 7 new letters, starting at the head
