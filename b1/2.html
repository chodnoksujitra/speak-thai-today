The same answers as in your book, the right way up. Tap a line to show it. Finish the page first!

p.25 Guess first

They are 5 different words: kaa stuck · kàa galangal · kâa price · káa trade · kǎa leg. Only the melody (tone) is different.

p.25 Warm-up

1 eye / grandpa · 2 no air · 3 five · 4 no

p.26

1 a hotel · 2 both · Hear it first: dips, then goes up = 5 · goes up, then drops = 3

p.27 Mini-check

kǎa rising · kaa mid · káa high · kàa low · kâa falling

p.28 Check

2 different words: kaa = stuck, kǎa = leg
