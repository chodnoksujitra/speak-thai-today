BOOK 1 · LESSON 7

High Letters

7 high-class letters · your first full sentence · at the som tam stall

AudioAnswersExtras
Book pages 81–9219 audio tracks

By the end of this lesson you can

  • Listen: hear mid vs rising (คา or ขา?), 4 out of 5
  • Read: 7 high-class letters, 14 words, a story and your first full sentence
  • Speak: order food, ask for a drink and say how spicy
  • Write: ข ถ ผ ส ฉ ฝ ห, and match each one with its partner

Answers

The same answers as in your book, the right way up. Tap a line to show it. Finish the page first!

p.81 Guess first

ขอกาแฟ kɔ̌ɔ gaa-fɛɛ = Coffee, please! (Grandpa wants coffee)

p.81 Warm-up

1 high: low class + short vowel · 2 TV · 3 kind · 4 mid

p.82

1 the seller: her ending goes high (pèt mǎi ká? = spicy?) · 2 plain water · 3 70 baht

p.83–84

Mini-check A: s · k · p · t · B: h · ch · f · Partners: ข–ค · ถ–ท · ผ–พ · ฝ–ฟ · ส–ซ · ห–ฮ · ฉ–ช

p.85

Rule 1 check: rising · Blend: ฝา fǎa · ผี pǐi · ใส sǎi · Odd one out: ฝี (a boil) · ชา · ฮา · ดู

p.86

Rule 2 check: low (pù) · Story: Grandpa asks for coffee, Aunt asks for soda. Grandpa watches TV. There’s a ghost on TV! Aunt looks for the light. Grandpa: “Look! The ghost is funny!”

p.87

พี่ or ผี: 1 pǐi · 2 pîi · 3 pîi · 4 pǐi

p.88

Sound Gym: 1 kǎa · 2 kaa · 3 hǎa · 4 hǎa · 5 fai · 6 fǎi · 7 kɔ̌ɔ · 8 kɔɔ

p.89 Pattern

1 ขอชา · 2 ขอกาแฟ · 3 ขอโซดา · 4 kɔ̌ɔ náam-bplàao · 5 your own (+ kráp / kâ)

p.90

Partners: ข ถ ผ ฝ ส ห ฉ · Listen and write: 1 หู · 2 ฝา · 3 ขอ

p.92 Quiz

1 rising · mid · rising · rising · mid · 2 clear · rub, mop · look! · 3 rising: high class + long vowel · 4 ข · ส · 5 หา · 6 sôm-dtam nʉ̀ng, pèt nít-nɔ̀i kráp / kâ · 7 Soda, please.

p.85 Grandpa’s note

ขอ kɔ̌ɔ rises: high-class ข + long vowel, no mark, no final. กาแฟ and ตา are mid.
