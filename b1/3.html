Short and Long

3 letters · 5 new vowel sounds · sorry, excuse me, it’s OK

By the end of this lesson you can

  • Listen: hear short and long vowels, 4 out of 5 times
  • Read: 3 new letters, 5 new vowel sounds (ะ ิ ุ ู ออ), 12 real words
  • Speak: say sorry and excuse me, and answer with “it’s OK”
  • Write: จ บ อ from memory, and words you hear

Answers

The same answers as in your book, the right way up. Tap a line to show it. Finish the page first!

p.35 Guess first

ปู bpuu = crab. It’s the crab tank.

p.35 Warm-up

1 rising · 2 kâ · 3 good · 4 don’t read it (today it becomes a real letter!)

p.36

1 Alex · 2 to call the waiter · 3 dâai kâ · Short or long: pair 1 long first (ดู ดุ) · pair 2 short first (ติ ตี) · pair 3 long first (กา กะ) · pair 4 short first (kâo kâao)

p.37 Mini-check

บ b · จ j · อ silent · ป bp

p.38

Rule 1 check: long · Rule 2 check: low (bì) · Blend: ปู bpuu · บิ bì

p.39

Spot: 7 × บ · อ: อา silent · กอ vowel · อีกา silent · ตอ vowel · Sound Gym: 1 short (ดุ) · 2 long (ปู) · 3 short (จะ) · 4 long (ตี) · 5 short (กะ) · 6 long (ดู) · 7 long (kâao) · 8 short (kâo)

p.41 Pattern

1 kɔ̌ɔ-tôot · 2 mâi bpen rai · 3 kɔ̌ɔ-tôot · 4 mâi bpen rai · 5 dâai · 6 bpai roong-rɛɛm

p.42

From memory: จ · บ · อ · ป · Listen and write: 1 ปู · 2 ดุ · 3 อา · 4 ตอ

p.44 Quiz

1 long, short, short, long, short (ดู ติ จะ ปู ดุ) · 2 crab, look, aunt/uncle · 3 low: mid-class + short vowel, no mark, no final · 4 silent · 5 check page 37 · 6 จะ · 7 mâi bpen rai kráp / kâ
