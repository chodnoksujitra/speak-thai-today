By the end of this lesson you can

  • Listen: hear the difference between sà-wàt-dii kráp and sà-wàt-dii kâ
  • Read: 4 letters and 7 real words, with no English letters
  • Speak: say hello politely, with the right ending for you
  • Write: ก ด ต ป from memory, starting at the right place
