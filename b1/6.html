Air or No Air

7 new letters · 2 rules · prices from 11 to 100 · at the market

AudioAnswersExtras

By the end of this lesson you can

  • Listen: hear prices from 11 to 100 and write them in 1-2-3, 8 out of 10
  • Read: 7 new letters, 13 words and a story
  • Speak: ask “How much is this?” and say a price
  • Write: ค ช ท พ ฟ ซ ฮ, starting at the head
