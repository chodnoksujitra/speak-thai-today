Counting Things and Money

Shopping for Gifts · counting words (classifiers) · hundreds to millions · a receipt · the Sunday Walking Street

AudioAnswers
Book pages 79–9015 audio tracks

By the end of this lesson you can

  • Count: count things the Thai way: กาแฟสองแก้ว
  • Numbers: read and say big prices: พัน · หมื่น · แสน · ล้าน
  • Pay: ask the total, ask about cards, get a receipt
  • Check: read a receipt and check your change

Read: gifts for home (p. 84)

T/F: F (three shirts) · T · T · Questions: 1) his mum and dad · 2) three · 3) 250 baht · Story: On Sunday I went to the walking street with June. I bought souvenirs for my mum and dad. Two bags, three shirts. 1,250 baht altogether. This shop doesn't take cards. I gave 1,500 in cash. The seller gave me 250 baht change. Then we had two coffees.

Read: gifts for home (talk) (p. 84)

1. Hello. Have a look first. 2. How much is this bag? 3. 400 baht each. 4. Two bags, please. And three shirts. 5. The shirts are 150 each. 6. How much altogether? 7. 1,250 baht altogether. 8. Do you take cards? 9. No. Cash or transfer. 10. Cash. Here is 1,500. A receipt, please. 11. Here is 250 change. 12. (At a coffee stall) Two coffees, please. ✗ 13. Two glasses of coffee, not two people. ✓
