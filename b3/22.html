BOOK 3 · LESSON 22

Wants, Needs and Help

At the Pharmacy · want to · have to · should · can · please help · a stomachache

AudioAnswers
Book pages 41–5215 audio tracks

By the end of this lesson you can

  • Say it: say what is wrong: stomachache, headache, fever, sore throat, allergic to…
  • Choose: use อยาก (want) · ต้อง (must) · ควร (should)
  • Can / can’t: put ได้ after the verb: ไปได้ · ไปไม่ได้
  • Ask for help: ask politely with ช่วย … หน่อย, and understand simple advice

Read: a quiet day (p. 46)

T/F: T · F · T · Questions: 1 no · 2 June · 3 take medicine and rest · Story: Today I'm sick. I have a stomachache. I can't go anywhere. June takes me to the pharmacy. The pharmacist asks if I have a fever. I don't have a fever. She gives me some medicine. I have to take the medicine and rest. I want to get better soon.

Read: a quiet day (talk) (p. 46)

1. Alex, what's wrong? Are you sick? 2. I have a stomachache. I ate very spicy som tam. 3. Shall we go to the pharmacy? It's close by. 4. Hello. What's the problem? 5. (OK Thai, but not how you ask a pharmacist) 6. Say “Do I need to take medicine?” 7. Do I need to take medicine? ✓ 8. Do you have a fever? An upset stomach? 9. No fever, but a slightly upset stomach. 10. Take two of these. You should drink lots of water and rest. 11. Thank you. Can I eat som tam? 12. You shouldn't yet.
