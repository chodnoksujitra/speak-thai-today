BOOK 3 · LESSON 27

Ordering and Comparing

Eating Out and Bargaining · more than · the most · the same · “no ___, please” · bargaining

AudioAnswers
Book pages 103–11415 audio tracks

By the end of this lesson you can

  • Order: order food with limits: ไม่ใส่ถั่ว · ไม่เผ็ดมาก
  • Compare: compare things: ถูกกว่า · อร่อยที่สุด · เท่ากัน
  • Bargain: ask for a lower price, politely: ลดได้ไหม
  • Recommend: tell a friend the best place to eat

Read: lunch and a lower price (p. 108)

T/F: T · F (this shop is better) · T · Questions: 1) at this shop in Chiang Mai, not Bangkok · 2) he is allergic · 3) แพงไป ลดได้ไหม · Story: Today I ate khao soi with June. This shop's khao soi is more delicious than in Bangkok. I had no peanuts, because I'm allergic to peanuts. Then we went to the market. I said, “Too expensive, can you lower the price?” The seller gave me a lower price: two for 250 baht.

Read: lunch and a lower price (talk) (p. 108)

1. What would you like? 2. Chicken khao soi, please, and one Thai papaya salad. No peanuts, please: I am allergic to peanuts. 3. Do you want the papaya salad spicy? 4. Not too spicy. And one lime juice, no sugar. 5. Eat here or to go? 6. Eat here. 7. (Later) I am full. The bill, please. 8. (At the market) How much is this one? 9. 150. 10. The most expensive. ✗ 11. Not “the most expensive”. Say “too expensive, can you lower the price?” 12. It is too expensive. If I buy two, can you lower the price? ✓ 13. Two for 250. A special price.

Sound Gym and real-speed listening (p. 109)

Sound Gym: 1 ผัก · 2 พัก · 3 พัก · 4 ผัก · Real speed: 1) Thai papaya salad + pork khao soi · 2) spicy, vegetables · 3) to go (จูนขอส้มตำไทย ไม่เผ็ด แล้วก็ข้าวซอยหมู ไม่ใส่ผัก ห่อนะคะ = A Thai papaya salad, not spicy, and a pork khao soi with no vegetables, to go, please.)
