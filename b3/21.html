BOOK 3 · LESSON 21

Saying No Politely

At the Night Market · four ways to say “no” · not yet · a tuk-tuk

AudioAnswers
Book pages 29–4015 audio tracks

By the end of this lesson you can

  • Say no: with 4 kinds of “not”: ไม่ · ไม่ใช่ · ไม่ได้ · ยังไม่
  • Choose: use ไม่ with a verb or a describing word, and ไม่ใช่ with a noun
  • Be polite: say no to a seller or a tuk-tuk with a smile: “no thanks”, “just looking”
  • Message: say no to a friend on LINE without hurting anyone
