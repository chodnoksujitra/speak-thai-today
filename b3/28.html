Final Project 1 · Read Alex's whole story (p. 120)

1) America · 2) June · 3) by train · 4) order food, give a driver directions · 5) come back to Chiang Mai · Story: My name is Alex. I am from America. I came to study Thai in Bangkok. I could not read Thai, but June helped me every day. Then we went to Chiang Mai by train. I went to June's home. I went to a temple on the mountain. I can order food by myself. I can give a driver directions by myself. Today I can speak Thai. I am very impressed. Next time I will come back to Chiang Mai.

Final Project 1 · Read Alex's whole story (talk) (p. 120)

1. Alex, your Thai is really good now. 2. Thank you. I practise speaking Thai every day. 3. Will you come back to Chiang Mai? 4. Yes, I will. Next year I want to bring my family. 5. Thanks for everything. ✗ 6. When you talk to my mum and dad, add “kráp”. 7. Thank you for everything. I am really impressed. ✓ 8. It is nothing, dear. (= you are welcome) 9. Have a safe trip. Good luck. 10. I will miss you. See you next time. 11. I will miss everyone. See you again.
