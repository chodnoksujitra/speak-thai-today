BOOK 3 · LESSON 20

Asking Questions

At June’s Family Guesthouse · what, where, who, when, why, how · answer with the verb

AudioAnswers
Book pages 17–2815 audio tracks

By the end of this lesson you can

  • Ask: ask with 8 question words: what, where, who, when, why, how, how many, and yes/no
  • Build: put the question word where the answer goes: “name what?”
  • Answer: answer a ___ไหม question with the verb, not “yes”
  • Check in: ask for a room, then say how many nights and how many people

Read: a room for Alex (p. 22)

T/F: T · F (he has no nickname) · F (he is very hungry) · Questions: 1 yes, but Mum has one free room for him · 2 no · 3 his surname · Story: June's family has a guesthouse in Chiang Mai. Today the rooms are full, but Mum has one free room for me. Mum asks me what my nickname is. I don't have a nickname. June says “Alex” is fine. Dad asks what my surname is. I say Smith. I have a lot of questions, but I'm very hungry. Mum says there is food at home.
