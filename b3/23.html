Build your own sentences (p. 57)

1 เมื่อวานผมไปวัด · 2 กำลังกิน · 3 ถอดรองเท้าแล้ว · 4 พรุ่งนี้จะไป · 5 เหนื่อยแต่สนุก · 6 เหนื่อยเพราะเดินเยอะ · Pattern: 1 กำลัง · 2 แล้ว · 3 จะ · 4 เคย · 5 แล้วก็ · 6 ถ้า

Read: Alex's day on the mountain (p. 58)

T/F: F · T · T · Questions: 1 by car, then they walked · 2 tired, but it was fun · 3 go to the market · Story: This morning June and I went up the mountain. We went up by car and then walked up to the temple. At the temple you must take off your shoes (to go inside). I took a lot of photos. Now I'm tired, but it was fun. Tomorrow I'll go to the market. Next year I want to bring my family to Chiang Mai.

Read: Alex's day on the mountain (talk) (p. 58)

1. Alex, where did you go today? 2. I went up the mountain, to the temple on the mountain. 3. Was it fun? 4. A lot of fun, but I'm tired because I walked up a lot. 5. Have you ever been to a Thai temple? 6. Yes. I've been to a temple in Bangkok. 7. At the temple, did you take off your shoes? 8. Yes. June told me already. 9. Are you going to the market tomorrow? 10. (“Tomorrow I already went.”) ✗ 11. Say “Tomorrow I will go.” 12. I'll go tomorrow, if I'm not tired. ✓

Sound Gym and real-speed listening (p. 59)

Sound Gym: 1 ข้าว · 2 เขา · 3 เข้า · 4 ข้าว · Real speed: 1 June · 2 what is he doing now · is he going out tomorrow · 3 the market (อเล็กซ์ ตอนนี้กำลังทำอะไร พรุ่งนี้จะไปเที่ยวไหม ถ้าไม่เหนื่อยไปตลาดกับจูนนะ = Alex, what are you doing now? Are you going out tomorrow? If you're not tired, come to the market with me.)
