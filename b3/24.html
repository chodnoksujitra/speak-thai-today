Time and Days

A Bus Ticket and a Dinner Plan · spoken time and the 24-hour clock · the 7 days · booking a bus

By the end of this lesson you can

  • Tell time: say the time the Thai way: ตี · โมงเช้า · บ่าย · ทุ่ม
  • Read: read 24-hour times on tickets and timetables (20.00, 14.30)
  • Days: say the 7 days of the week
  • Meet: make a plan with a friend: day + time

Read: Alex's last days (p. 72)

T/F: F (it arrives at 6 am) · T · T · Questions: 1 Friday, 8 pm · 2 10 hours · 3 dinner with June and her friend · Story: On Friday I'm going back to Bangkok. The bus leaves at 8 pm. It arrives at 6 am. On Thursday at 8 pm I'll have dinner with June and her friend. I have to be on time. I don't want to be late.

Read: Alex's last days (talk) (p. 72)

1. Hello. Where to? 2. To Bangkok, on Friday. What time does the bus leave? 3. There is a bus at 8 pm and one at 9:30 pm. 4. What time does the 8 pm bus arrive? 5. It arrives at 6 am. 6. The 8 pm one, please. 7. Alex, do you want to have dinner with my friend on Thursday? 8. Yes. What time? 9. 8 pm. 10. 2 pm, right? ✗ 11. Not 2 pm. 8 pm. 12. OK. 8 pm. See you. ✓

Sound Gym and real-speed listening (p. 73)
