BOOK 3 · LESSON 19

Making Sentences

On the Train · word order · describing words come after · no need to say “I”

AudioAnswers
Book pages 5–1615 audio tracks

By the end of this lesson you can

  • Speak: introduce yourself in 3 sentences: name · country · job (+ age)
  • Build: put the describing word after the noun: big house = house big
  • Listen: answer short questions the Thai way, without “I”
  • Write: type and send your first Thai LINE message
